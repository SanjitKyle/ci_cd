🏠 Week 3: Home AWS Setup

Follow this checklist when you get home to your personal AWS account!

Step 1: Login

Log into your Personal AWS Root Account on your home computer.

Step 2: Navigate to IAM

In the top search bar of AWS, type IAM and click the first result to go to the Identity and Access Management dashboard.

Step 3: Add Identity Provider

  • On the left-hand menu, click on Identity providers.
  • Click the blue button in the top right that says Add provider.
  • Select the OpenID Connect circle (Do NOT select SAML).

Step 4: Fill in the Details

Copy and paste these exact values into the boxes:

Provider URL:
https://token.actions.githubusercontent.com

(Note: If AWS shows a button that says "Get thumbprint", click it!)

Audience:
sts.amazonaws.com

Step 5: Save

Scroll to the bottom of the page and click the Add provider button.

✅ Done!
